Before we start

Meet the cyber defenders

Every day, sneaky people online try to break into computers, steal accounts and trick kids out of their stuff. Cyber defenders are the good guys who stop them. Today, you get to train like one.

Your guide today: FedXI'm a cyber defender. My job is finding the bad guys hiding inside computers and kicking them out before they cause trouble.

Four kinds of defenders

The lookoutsThey watch the alarms day and night. When something weird happens on a computer, they're the first to spot it.
The detectivesThey hunt for bad guys who are hiding, following clues before anything goes wrong. (That's me!)
The firefightersWhen an attack happens, they rush in, stop it from spreading, and fix what got broken.
The buildersThey design the locks, walls and passwords that keep bad guys out in the first place.
